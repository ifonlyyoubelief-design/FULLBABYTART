      Read before you join

      Joining costs the entry fee (about 20 cents) in Baby Tart and the fee is not refunded once the round is paid out. Where the fees go: 50% is the prize pool and 50% goes to the Baby Tart team marketing wallet. The prize pool is split equally between the two games, and in each game the top 10 players get 30%, 20%, 14%, 10%, 8%, 6%, 4%, 3%, 3% and 2%. If a round has fewer than 10 players in a game, the unclaimed prize money carries over to the next round. Each wallet joins a round once and can play as many times as it likes. If the winners are not submitted within 3 days of the round ending, every player can take their entry fee back. Signing a score is free and never moves tokens. Skill contests with entry fees are restricted in some countries, so only join if it is allowed where you live.

      How to claim, step by step

      The screenshots use Bitget Wallet. MetaMask, Trust Wallet and other wallets work the same way: open the wallet's built-in browser, go to www.babytart.com and follow steps 4 to 6.

      Before you start

      Use a separate wallet, not your main one, and make sure it holds a little BNB for gas. Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone.

      1. Open your wallet app

        Open your wallet and tap the search bar at the top of the screen.

      2. Enter the website

        Type www.babytart.com into the search bar and tap the search key on your keyboard.

      3. Confirm the redirect

        Your wallet warns that you are opening a third-party site. Tick the box and tap Confirm.

      4. Use my wallet

        Scroll down to Claim your Tartswap rewards and tap Use my wallet. Approve the connection in your wallet if it asks.

      5. Check, then claim

        Your address fills in. Tap Check rewards to see your amount, then tap Claim Tartswap.

      6. Confirm in your wallet

        Check it says BNB Chain and a tiny network fee, then tap Confirm. Your Tartswap arrives in your wallet.

      Rewards show in your wallet as TART. Claiming costs a very small amount of BNB for gas, so keep a little BNB in the wallet.

      How rewards work

      1. Buy and hold

        Buy Baby Tart and keep it in your wallet.

      2. Tax builds rewards

        Tax is 4% on buys and 4% on sells. Half of it buys Tartswap for holders and half goes to the marketing wallet.

      3. Claim any time

        Your share of the Tartswap builds up automatically. Paste your wallet above and claim whenever you like.
